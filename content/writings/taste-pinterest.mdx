back amidst middle school was i on this app called pinterest and could open my eyes to the phenomena of pinterest boards galore.

in a way, it was a fractional segment of manifesting your dream life.

i’d beg to argue that curating is equally as diluted as straight up consumption, but then again, you can boast the point that the art of curating is put in front of the action that you will actually do something of the same artistic nature.

id think that’s maybe why every board of mine ends in “inspo”.

every pin is scrapped together to produce new segments of what has been perceived to be tasteful, and the cyclic nature repeats.

taste is a careful spin on pre-existing idealizations of a life you want to embody in some segment. the haircut you want. the nails you show to your nail lady. the fits you want to elevate.

i hope that every pinterest curator possesses the same demeanor of swag to put their own rendition of art down to pixels for the world to see.

my instagram is completely nonchalant, but pinterest? i curated my own flavor, capturable by life’s art of experience. it might not be perfect, but it’s me. fin.
